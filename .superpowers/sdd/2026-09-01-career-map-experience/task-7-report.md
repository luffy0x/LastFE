## What changed

- Added a Playwright behavior regression at exactly 768×900 that reads the five rendered `.region-label` DOM bounds and requires every edge to remain inside the viewport.
- Changed the shared SVG viewport adaptation in `StrategicMap` so viewports at least 641px wide with a square or portrait aspect ratio use `xMidYMid meet`; mobile and landscape viewports retain `xMidYMid slice`.
- Regenerated and inspected the four required snapshot targets. The 768 baseline changed; the 375, 1024, and 1440 images were regenerated by the command and remained byte-identical.

## Why it changed

The page-level overflow assertion could not detect content clipped inside the SVG. At 768×900, `viewBox="0 0 1000 600"` with `xMidYMid slice` scaled by viewport height and exposed only logical x≈244–756, while labels near x=228, x=232, and x=759 crossed the SVG viewport.

The fix changes the responsive map viewport policy at the common cause. It does not hide labels, move individual anchors, change label font size, or add horizontal page scrolling. The 375px composition retains the dense central map plus equivalent territory-list navigation, while 1024px and 1440px retain their existing landscape composition.
